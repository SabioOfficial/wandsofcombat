**Welcome** to the world of _**Wands of Combat** (WoC)_. In this mod, you'll have the opportunity to craft **unique** and _powerful_ wands or discover _ancient_ and **mind-blowing** wands. This can range from wands that **freeze your enemies** to wands that allow you to **latch onto players and mobs**!

> This mod is under **heavy development**. The current version of the mod **will be** changed _**significantly**_ over the updates it will receive.

![hr](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/f9ef6b8eb5bc66fcf87cd276409f6705a1fbc79b.png)

![Features](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/4517bf16c9f8721c3b11070eeab1e32dd133beb1.png)

- 5 **unique** wands
- 16 **different** abilities
- **Lots** of **new** mechanics
- A **Mana System**
- Potions + **Custom** Effects
- In-game **ability** descriptions
